# Should Garage Floor Coating Companies Market During Tax Refund Season?

**Meta Description:** Yes, market garage floor coating in tax refund season. Filing opens in late January and most e-filed refunds arrive within 21 days, right as spring planning starts.

---

Yes. Garage floor coating companies should be running ads from February through April, because that is when homeowners plan spring projects and when many of them have a refund landing. The IRS opens filing season in late January, most e-filed refunds with direct deposit arrive within 21 days, and IRS filing season statistics have put the average refund around $3,000 in recent years. Market the spring project honestly, not the refund, and book estimates on a schedule your slab temperatures and crew can actually meet.

## Key takeaways

- Tax refund season and spring project planning overlap almost exactly, from mid February through April.
- Refunds arrive in waves: early e-filers first, then households claiming the EITC or Additional Child Tax Credit, whose refunds by law cannot go out before mid February.
- Sell the spring garage project, not the refund. "Use your refund" ads feel like pressure, and the homeowner who planned the project already knows how they are paying for it.
- Warm markets can install through the whole refund window; cold markets should book estimates and take deposits now for installs once the slab is warm enough.
- This page is about marketing timing and messaging, not tax advice. Never tell a homeowner anything about their taxes.

## When does tax refund season actually happen?

Only a few dates matter for a floor coating calendar:

- **Late January:** the IRS opens filing season.
- **Within 21 days of filing:** most e-filed refunds with direct deposit arrive. A homeowner who files in the first days of the season can see money by mid to late February.
- **Mid February:** by law, refunds claiming the Earned Income Tax Credit or the Additional Child Tax Credit cannot be issued before this point. Those households get their money later than early filers.
- **Through March and April:** people who file later keep receiving refunds as they file.

So refund money does not land on one day. It spreads across February, March, and April, and demand from this window builds rather than spiking for a week.

IRS filing season statistics have put the average refund around $3,000 in recent years. For comparison, a two car garage is roughly 400 to 500 square feet, and garage floor coating sells for $5 to $8 per square foot on our [floor coating benchmarks](/floor-coating-benchmarks). That math is why some homeowners pick this window for the garage. It is not a reason to assume any homeowner has spare money.

## Why does refund season line up with spring floor coating demand?

Because outside Florida, the Gulf Coast, and the Carolinas, floor coating demand is strong spring through fall and drops in winter. February through April is when homeowners in most of the country start looking at the garage again. They are cleaning it out, planning the year, and deciding which project gets done first.

Refund season does not create that demand. It sits on top of it. A homeowner already thinking about the garage now has a clearer picture of what they can spend.

## What should the ads say, and what should they avoid?

Talk about the project, not the homeowner's money.

Ads that say "Got your refund? Spend it here!" read as pushy, and they can feel predatory to a family counting on that money for something else. The homeowner who planned a garage project already knows how they are paying for it. Your job is to show the finished floor and make the next step easy.

| Avoid | Use instead | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Turn your tax refund into a new garage floor" | "Planning your spring garage project? Get a firm price now." | Frames a project they already plan, not their money |
| "Don't let your refund disappear" | "Book your estimate before the spring schedule fills" | Real urgency about your calendar, not guilt |
| "Limited time refund special" running all season | One honest offer tied to install dates | Fake deadlines cost trust at the estimate |
| Any claim about deductions or tax benefits | No tax claims at all | You are not a tax advisor |
| Stock photos of cash or checks | A real before and after of a local garage | On Meta, the creative is the targeting |

For a seasonal hook, use the season itself: spring cleanout, or the floor done before summer. Those are true for every homeowner, refund or not. More on creative is in [Meta ads for floor coating contractors](/blog/meta-ads-for-floor-coating-contractors).

> **From the field:** A homeowner planning a spring project usually has questions before they want a price: epoxy versus polyaspartic, how long the install takes, what it roughly costs. Across client accounts, landing page leads book better than native Meta lead form leads, and a first call that answers those questions is what turns the lead into an estimate.

## What does a February to April plan look like week by week?

Warm markets are Florida, the Gulf Coast, and the Carolinas. Cold markets are everywhere the slab gets cold in winter.

| Week | What is happening | Warm markets | Cold markets |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Late January | IRS opens filing season | Ads running, refresh creative for spring | Build creative and landing page, line up who calls leads |
| Feb week 1 | Early filers waiting on refunds | Normal budget, spring project messaging | Launch ads, book estimates for spring installs |
| Feb week 2 | Early e-filed refunds can start arriving | Hold budget, call every lead within a minute | Take deposits to hold spring install dates |
| Feb week 3 | EITC and ACTC refunds can now be issued | Watch install backlog, protect estimate time | Keep booking estimates, sell the spring date |
| Feb week 4 | Refunds keep arriving | Full calendar check, adjust price if booked out | Check slab temps against your spec sheet |
| Mar weeks 1 to 2 | Later filers file and wait | Steady budget, fresh creative | Raise budget as demand climbs |
| Mar weeks 3 to 4 | Spring planning in full swing | Steady, keep reminders tight | First installs where your system allows |
| Apr weeks 1 to 2 | Later filers keep filing | Normal season operations | Full spring push, watch callback speed |
| Apr weeks 3 to 4 | Refunds still arriving for later filers | Normal season operations | Spring demand carries on without the refund boost |

In cold markets, the refund often arrives before the slab is warm enough for epoxy. Many epoxy systems want the slab comfortably above 50°F, while many polyaspartic and polyurea systems are rated to install colder. Your manufacturer's spec sheet is the rule. If you can only install epoxy, sell the estimate and the spring date now and install when the slab is ready. The [polyaspartic vs epoxy](/blog/polyaspartic-vs-epoxy-garage-floors) breakdown covers what a cold rated system buys you.

## How is this different in warm and cold markets?

**Warm markets** book year round, so refund season is extra demand on a calendar that is already running. The risk is overbooking. Keep the one minute callback, keep reminders tight, and raise your price before you let estimates sit weeks out. Our [spring rush playbook](/guides/floor-coating-spring-rush-playbook) covers the signals.

**Cold markets** have the opposite problem. Demand and money show up before the weather does. The winning move is to book estimates in February and March, sell a real install date, and take a deposit to hold it. If you spend the winter on indoor work, our guide to [keeping a coating crew busy in winter](/guides/keep-floor-coating-crew-busy-in-winter) covers that side.

## How much should I spend during refund season?

Spend at your normal spring level, starting earlier. If you are new to Meta, a common starting budget is $50 to $100 a day. Increase it only if your callbacks stay inside a minute and your estimate calendar has room. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is tax refund season a good time to advertise garage floor coating?

Yes. Refund season runs from late January into April, which overlaps the start of spring planning in most markets. Many homeowners decide on a garage floor coating project in that window. Advertise the spring project, not the refund, and make sure every lead gets called fast.

### Should my epoxy flooring ads mention tax refunds?

It is better not to. Ads built around "use your refund" can feel like pressure, especially to families counting on that money. Show the finished epoxy or polyaspartic floor, talk about the spring project, and let the homeowner decide how to pay. Never make claims about taxes.

### When do tax refunds arrive?

The IRS opens filing season in late January, and most e-filed refunds with direct deposit arrive within 21 days. By law, refunds claiming the EITC or the Additional Child Tax Credit cannot be issued before mid February. Refunds keep arriving through March and April as people file.

### Can I install a concrete coating in February in a cold market?

It depends on your system and your slab. Many epoxy systems want the slab comfortably above 50°F, and many polyaspartic and polyurea systems are rated to install colder. Follow the manufacturer's spec sheet. If you cannot install yet, book the estimate and take a deposit for a spring install date.

### Do floor coating companies in Florida need a refund season plan?

Less than cold markets do. Florida, the Gulf Coast, and the Carolinas book floor coating year round, so refund season adds demand to a running calendar. The plan there is avoiding overbooking.
