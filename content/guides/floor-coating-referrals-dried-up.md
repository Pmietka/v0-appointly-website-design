# Word of Mouth Dried Up for My Floor Coating Business. What Do I Do Now?

**Meta Description:** When floor coating referrals dry up, work past customers in week 1, reviews and Google in week 2, then Meta ads with a landing page and calls within a minute.

---

When floor coating referrals dry up, spend week 1 contacting every past customer, week 2 fixing your reviews and Google Business Profile, and weeks 3 and 4 launching Meta ads that show real local floors and send homeowners to a landing page. The step that decides whether it works is the first call: 60 to 70% of floor coating leads should become a booked estimate when ads are high intent and every lead is called fast. Keep doing everything that earned referrals, because the goal is a second channel, not a replacement for good work.

## Key takeaways

- Referrals rarely stop because your work got worse. They slow down when the circle of people who know you has already bought.
- Your past customers are the fastest source of new work in week 1, because they already trust you and can see your floor every day.
- Reviews and a complete Google Business Profile do not create demand on their own, but they decide whether a stranger who finds you trusts you.
- A paid channel can replace referral volume within weeks, but only if every lead gets a call within minutes and a clear next step.
- About 90% of homeowners answer when called within one minute of submitting a form, and the answer rate falls fast after that.
- Run the new channel alongside referrals. Do not stop asking past customers just because ads are running.

## Why did my floor coating referrals stop?

Usually because your referral circle ran out of garages, not because anything broke. Word of mouth travels through your customers' neighbors, coworkers, and family. After a few seasons, most of the people in that circle who wanted a coated floor have one. Other times a builder or realtor who sent you work moves on, and one source going quiet takes half your calendar with it.

The long term case for not depending on referrals is in [how to get floor coating jobs without relying on word of mouth](/blog/floor-coating-jobs-without-word-of-mouth). This page is the emergency version: the next 30 days, when the phone has already gone quiet.

## What does a 30 day plan to replace referral work look like?

Work in this order. The cheapest, fastest sources come first.

| Week | Focus | What you do | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Past customers | Call or text every past customer, check on their floor, ask who else they know, offer to look at basements, patios, or a second garage | The fastest estimates, from people who already trust you |
| Week 2 | Reviews and Google | Ask happy customers for reviews, fill every field on your Google Business Profile, load real before and after photos | Trust when a stranger looks you up before the estimate |
| Week 3 | Meta ads and a landing page | Build ads around photos of floors you installed locally, send traffic to a landing page with your photos, reviews, and a short form | New homeowners who never heard of you |
| Week 4 | Speed and follow up | Call every lead within minutes, offer two estimate times, confirm and remind every booked estimate | Leads that turn into estimates that show up |

## What should I do in week 1 with past customers?

Call them. Do not send a mass email and hope. A past customer sees your floor every time they park, and many have a basement, patio, or shop that needs the same work. On each call:

- **Check on the floor.** Ask how it is holding up. If something needs attention, schedule it. That alone earns goodwill.
- **Ask about other spaces.** Basements, porches, and shop floors are the easiest add on work you will ever book.
- **Ask who else they know.** Be specific: "Has anybody on your street asked about your garage?"
- **Ask for a review if they have not left one.** Happy customers are the reviews you need for week 2.

## How do reviews and Google help in week 2?

They help strangers trust you. With referrals, a friend vouched for you. When a stranger finds you through an ad, your reviews and profile are the friend.

Fill out every field on your Google Business Profile, add your service area, and load photos of real floors, not stock images. Then ask for reviews while the customer is standing on the finished floor. The full setup is in our guide to [Google reviews for floor coating contractors](/blog/google-reviews-floor-coating-contractors), and [NFC review cards](/blog/nfc-review-cards-floor-coating-contractors) make asking at the walkthrough easy.

Do not expect a profile to fill your calendar in two weeks. It is the foundation for week 3, not the replacement for referrals.

## How do I set up Meta ads in week 3 without wasting money?

Get three things right and most of the waste goes away.

1. **Show your own local floors.** On Meta, the creative is the targeting. An ad with a real garage floor you installed in your area, shot on your phone, attracts homeowners who want that floor. A stock photo attracts anyone.
2. **Send people to a landing page, not the native lead form.** A landing page produces better leads than Meta's built in form. It asks for a little more effort, so the people who fill it out are more serious.
3. **Start at a budget you can hold.** The starting Meta budget we see for a new contractor is $50 to $100 a day. Pick a number you can keep running for the full month, because stopping and starting resets your learning.

Keep your service area tight. Paid leads cost the same whether the homeowner is down the road or across the county, so set the ad area to where you want to drive. More on the ad side in [Meta ads for floor coating contractors](/blog/meta-ads-for-floor-coating-contractors).

## Why does the first call matter so much in week 4?

Because a paid lead is not a referral. A referral calls you already sold. A paid lead filled out a form while scrolling, and they may have filled out two others that night.

> **From the field:** Our [floor coating benchmarks](/floor-coating-benchmarks) put the booked rate at 60 to 70% of leads when ads are high intent, refreshed often, send traffic to a landing page, and every lead is called fast. That is why a paid channel can replace referral volume quickly when the follow up is right. When the follow up is slow, the same ads look like they are not working, and the owner turns them off right before they would have paid off.

The first call has three jobs: reach the homeowner while they are still thinking about their garage, explain the product so they understand why a professional coating is different from a kit, and book a time that fits your route. Offer two times instead of asking when works. Then confirm at booking and remind 24 hours and 1 hour out. About 80% of booked estimates show with no reminders and about 95% show with them.

If your leads come in but go silent, our guide on [why floor coating leads don't answer](/guides/why-floor-coating-leads-dont-answer) walks through the causes and a call and text cadence.

## How do I keep referrals going while I add a new channel?

Keep doing what earned them, then make it easy to refer you:

- **Ask at the walkthrough**, not by text a week later.
- **Leave something behind.** A card with your number and a photo of their own floor.
- **Track where every job came from.** Write "referral," "past customer," "ad," or "Google" next to every estimate. In 30 days you will know which channel is carrying you.

A business with two channels can survive one of them going quiet. A business with one cannot.

## What if I do not have time to run all of this myself?

For most owners, week 4 breaks first. Nobody on a grinder can call a lead within a minute at 2 p.m. on a Tuesday. If that is your gap, read [how to get floor coating jobs without chasing leads](/guides/floor-coating-leads-without-chasing) before you spend on ads.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did my floor coating referrals slow down?

Most floor coating referrals slow down because the circle of people who know your past customers has already bought, or because one big referral source went quiet. It rarely means your work got worse. The fix is adding a channel that reaches homeowners outside that circle while you keep earning referrals.

### How fast can Meta ads replace word of mouth for an epoxy flooring company?

Meta ads can produce leads within days, but leads only replace referral work when they turn into estimates. When an epoxy or garage floor coating company runs high intent ads to a landing page and calls every lead fast, 60 to 70% of leads should become booked estimates. Slow follow up is the most common reason the channel looks like it failed.

### Should I contact past customers when concrete coating referrals dry up?

Yes, first. Past concrete coating customers already trust you, can see your work every day, and often have another space like a basement, patio, or shop floor. Call them, check on their floor, ask who else they know, and ask the happy ones for a review.

### Do I still need Google reviews if I am running ads?

Yes. A homeowner who finds your garage floor coating company through an ad will usually look you up before the estimate. Reviews and real photos on your Google Business Profile are what make a stranger trust you the way a referral used to.

### Will running ads hurt my referral business?

No. Ads reach homeowners outside your referral circle, and every polyaspartic or epoxy floor you install from an ad becomes a new source of referrals. Keep asking for referrals at every walkthrough while the ads run.
