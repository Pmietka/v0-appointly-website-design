## What does a floor coating setter actually do on the call?

Most agencies send you a lead and wish you luck. We call every lead ourselves, and we know how to talk to a homeowner about floor coating.

On a first call, our setter does four things:

1. **Answers fast.** The call goes out within minutes of the form submission, while the homeowner is still thinking about their garage.

3. **Qualifies the homeowner.** They own the property, they want coating work, they sit inside the service area, and they have a real project in mind.
4. **Books a time that fits the route.** The estimate lands on the contractor's calendar in a slot that works for the crew.
