## What happens on the call?

Most agencies send you a lead and wish you luck. We call every lead ourselves, and we know how to talk to a homeowner about floor coating.

Jacob makes every one of those calls himself, and every call is recorded and reviewed. On a first call, he does four things:

1. **Answers fast.** The homeowner gets a text and a call within one minute of the form submission, while the homeowner is still thinking about their garage.
2. **Educates on the product.** Coating systems, prep, cure time, and why a professional install holds up when a kit from the hardware store peels.
3. **Qualifies the homeowner on intent.** Their project goal, the look and material they want, and a real timeline. No real timeline means no booking.
4. **Books a time that fits the route.** The estimate lands on the contractor's calendar in a slot that works for the crew.

By the time our client shows up, the homeowner already understands the product and wants it.
